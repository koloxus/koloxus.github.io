Mantis

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computers.

Mantis is not a product or a service. It is one person's own software, running on their own machines, and it has no other users. There is nothing to sign up for here.

It can connect to its owner's Google accounts to show their calendar events beside their tasks, and to create, move, edit or delete those events when the owner does so in Mantis. It does nothing to the calendars on its own.

It can also show the owner’s Gmail, and send mail the owner writes in it.
