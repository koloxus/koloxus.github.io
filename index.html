Mantis

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computers.

Mantis is not a product or a service. It is one person's own software, running on their own machines, and it has no other users. There is nothing to sign up for here.

It can connect to its owner's Google account to read their calendar, so that their events can be shown beside their tasks. It only reads; it does not change anything in the account.
