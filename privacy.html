Mantis privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

Mantis is personal software used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers. It has no other users and offers no service to the public.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, Mantis reads the owner's Google Calendar events through Google's read-only calendar access, so they can be shown beside the owner's tasks. It does not create, change or delete anything in the Google account.

Where it goes

Anything Mantis reads from Google is kept only on the owner's own computers. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any other person or company, it is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any artificial intelligence model.

Mantis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps and services. Removing it there stops Mantis reading anything further, and the owner can delete what was already read from their own computers.
